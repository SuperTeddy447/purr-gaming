## Measured scene basis and proposal boundary

GodotAI live inspection on Godot 4.7.2 confirmed the locked scene, `Floor` at `(0,0)` with `z_index=-10`, `DepthSortedLayer.y_sort_enabled=true`, and camera `(320,650)`, zoom `1.8`, limits `(0,0)–(640,1320)`. Project portrait override is 540×960; existing tall profile is 539×1168. The real running locked viewport was 539×959. The floor script draws a 640×1320 room, top edge band `(0,72,640,84)`, side hints `(26,156,20,1110)` and `(594,156,20,1110)`, service hints `(50,194,252,252)` and `(311,225,265,236)`, and window hint `(565,360,40,172)`. Navigation's walkable rectangle is `(25,100,590,1190)` and object-owned footprints remain authoritative.

The six production envelopes below are **proposed art-fit bounds derived from those locked hints**, not newly locked gameplay geometry. If a final image cannot fit them without moving a root, slot, footprint or camera, reject/re-author the image or request an explicit spatial review. The wall/service/window overlap is deliberate: transparent cut-outs must align with the same opening; it does not authorize a larger window or changed circulation.

## Final Batch A inventory and slots

Every pivot is the **full source canvas top-left `(0,0)`**; `Sprite2D.centered=false`. Display size is independent of source pixels. All coordinates below are world units in the locked room. The empty staging `Texture`/`FrameTexture` children show exactly where a future imported PNG goes. RGBA8 is `width × height × 4 / 1,048,576` MiB, before mipmaps or GPU compression.

Five broad/narrow architecture sources use 1.5 source px per world unit; the window frame uses 4 px/world for its thin trim and pane edge. ## Component and ownership boundaries

- **A1 floor:** one opaque, quiet neutral illustrated surface. Warm floor material, restrained jade detail, subtle fixed threshold zoning are allowed. No table/chair/character cast shadows, sun streaks, lamp pools, wetness or time/season grade. Floor is not collision or navigation. Door passage stays visually open around `EntranceDoor (320,1165)`; the threshold treatment cannot paint closed leaves.
- **A2 fixed shell:** north and two unique side strips supply cream plaster, muted jade wainscot, warm wood and restrained brass. Left/right textures may visually meet the north wall, but no repeating floor pattern is hidden inside them. The right strip must have a clean, aligned transparent cut-out wherever it crosses the A3 view-through. The north wall carries a blank café-name mounting surface. These are behind gameplay, not giant foreground masks. No lamps, plants, movable shelf, chair, cat or fixed weather/season decoration.
- **A3 window frame:** the production frame is fitted to the locked right-side hint, not the 256×768 proof texture's display size. The view-through envelope is `(565,360,40,172)` world; on its 256×768 canvas this is approximately `(52,48)–(212,736)` source pixels. Its pane regions must be alpha-clear; thin structural mullions may occupy parts of that envelope. Frame structural pixels may surround it inside the 64×192 display rect. The **same frame PNG** must work with day/sunset/night/rain and region/season exterior data. `ExteriorBackdrop` is behind it; `WeatherOverlay`, `GlassOverlay`, and `WindowLightContribution` are separate runtime attachment slots. The future window controller clips/masks exterior and weather to the actual pane apertures. The service-wall and right-strip layers must be completely alpha-clear at their portions of the broader opening envelope. `WindowPerch (515,520)` and `Plant (530,680)` remain world objects; no perch, plant or cat pixels in A3. No permanent sky painting, rain streak, glow or beam in the frame.
- **A4 service wall:** one fixed, transparent registered layer links left counter/order and right prep bay through panel datum, quiet cabinetry/recess/trim, but no equipment pixels. Its world rect is `(48,188,536,280)`; within the locked floor hint, the right edge intersects the window hint and therefore requires a matched alpha opening, not a repositioned window. `CounterShell (160,380)`, `POSStation (90,235)`, `PastryCase (245,230)`, `EspressoStation (360,350)`, and `GrinderStation (485,350)` remain five separate PackedScene gameplay objects with their own stable IDs, slots, footprints, visual replacements and FX/camera markers. Keep `(310,285,235,180)` visually quiet for Worker+Espresso+Cup Brew/Cup shots; this is a review zone, not a new collider. Do not bake espresso steam, cup, readable menu or a counter-front mask here.

`CounterShell/VisualFrontOccluder` remains the **object-owned** front lip, above/below actors through the existing depth architecture. Batch A creates **no room-wide front occluder**. Architecture fixed layers sit behind `DepthSortedLayer`; object-local front visuals and floor-contact Y-sort handle occlusion. Any future small architectural front trim must be a separately justified spatial piece reviewed against actor traversal—not part of these six assets by default.

## Signage, entrance, atmosphere and later batches

Proposed blank text-safe surfaces, subject to real-art QA: main café-name `Rect2(82,98,176,38)` on north wall; compact changing menu `Rect2(340,190,164,34)` on the service-wall upper zone. The runtime text system owns café name, menu and localization. Keep the plaques blank, with low-contrast center, no pseudo-legible lettering; text clipping/perspective should be handled by surface-attached runtime nodes, not an environment-wide HUD approximation. If the text cannot read at 540×960 or either surface conflicts with a camera/actor, return the asset for revision; do not move the gameplay object beneath it.

The room-owned architecture may imply a doorway opening, but **door leaves and their animation** remain with the later independent `entrance_door` asset. A2 side trim may visually frame the threshold without closing it. Event OFF must still look like a complete café; event décor stays an object/variant. Location, season, time, weather and event profiles tint/light the neutral base and select small exterior/weather/decor variants. No country palette stereotype or one-image-per-state room. Floor and shell never switch for ordinary day/rain/night.

Batch A intentionally does not solve CounterShell hero art/front lip, POS, PastryCase, EspressoStation, GrinderStation, tables, chairs, CatBed, WindowPerch, Plant, ScratchPost, EntranceDoor leaves, event props, cat/character art, final signage text, runtime FX, actual exterior variants or phone performance. Their locked ownership remains as in the [object handoff](WILLICAT_HOME_V3_ART_HANDOFF_SPEC_LOCK_V1.md).

## Forge, placement and acceptance

Preserve immutable sources under `docs/source_assets/home_v3/production_batch_a_v1/` (`.gdignore`), then run the existing Forge `build-static` recipe. Opaque floor uses `--pivot FULL_CANVAS_TOP_LEFT --opaque --expected-size 960x1980`. Registered transparent fixed layers use the new **pixel-preserving** `--pivot FULL_CANVAS_TOP_LEFT --preserve-canvas --expected-size WxH`; Forge copies pixels byte-for-byte and records source/runtime hashes, canvas, visible alpha bounds and edge-touch flags. The ordinary crop/recenter `build-static` path is **for isolated props**, not Batch A room layers. Technical PASS is not art approval. On import, verify exact dimensions, origin, alpha holes, window mask, overlap/bleed seams, world size and RGBA8/mipmap cost. Forge flags lighting-neutrality and architectural ownership violations for human review; it never chooses scene coordinates.

Acceptance in the staging scene: all six art slots align without moving locked roots/anchors; no interactive pixels appear in static layers; five service objects remain independently replaceable; `WindowPerch`/Plant remain in front of a region/time/weather-neutral frame; Brew/Cup and both portrait profiles stay readable; door lane/event OFF remain clear; no stale opaque patches under the exterior; no giant front mask; camera/default/min/Brew/Cup views pass; source hashes stay unchanged. Finally repeat Godot parser, layout/architecture/camera tests, `git diff --check`, and a real-device imported-memory/frame-time test before production cutover. Human sign-off is required before marking any asset FINAL.
