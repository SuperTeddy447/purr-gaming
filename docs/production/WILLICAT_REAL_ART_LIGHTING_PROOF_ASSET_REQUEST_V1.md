## Shared export rules

All A–E runtime candidates are independent, full-canvas 8-bit RGBA PNGs with transparent outer corners and **no visible alpha touching the canvas edge**. A–D use neutral illustrated base lighting: rich painted material volume, hue variation, shallow self-shadow and subtle local AO are allowed; golden-hour wash, fixed blue night, long cast sunlight, furniture floor shadow, lamp pools on unrelated surfaces, wet whole-room grade, baked rays and permanent seasonal tint are forbidden. Elevated 3/4 / isometric-lite camera, matte storybook finish, consistent proportions. No character, machine, other furniture, signage text or room-wide background baked into a sample. All assets remain CANDIDATE until Godot/mobile visual review.

The existing [import-slot script](../../scripts/dev/atmosphere/real_art_proof_slot.gd) uses the exact PNG canvas and pixel pivot below. `world_scale` maps asset pixels to authored world units; a replacement changes only `candidate_texture` in its existing slot. A wrong canvas or any opaque perimeter pixel blocks captures. Expected visible bounds are **content bounds inside the transparent export**, with small tolerance for brush hairs but no clipping. Bounds are pixel coordinates `(left, top)–(right, bottom)`; all pivots are pixels from canvas top-left.

### A — architecture/material panel

Purpose: compare cream plaster, warm wood, muted deep jade trim, one restrained aged-brass detail and the floor junction in one *small* service-wall panel. Perspective is the same elevated 3/4 camera as Home V3, viewed toward the fixed service wall. Transparent outside the illustrated section; do not return a full café room or an opaque rectangle. Junction/pivot at `(700,600)` in the export; content may cross it onto a shallow floor strip. Painted plaster/wood joinery and subtle AO are allowed; **no** directional beam, permanent lamp contribution or cast prop shadow. Shadow ownership: intrinsic architectural AO in art; time/weather shadows belong to runtime. Runtime light ownership: scene CanvasModulate + restrained sun and local fixture/window lights. Normal map: **not requested for A** in this first controlled trial. Validate five materials remain identifiable in neutral/day/sunset/night/rain, no clipping or baked-light conflict, no visual obstruction of the worker.

## Import, Forge and rejection policy

Return immutable approved raw PNGs under `docs/source_assets/home_v3_real_art_proof_v1/` (protected by `.gdignore`) and candidate runtime copies under `assets/dev/home_v3_real_art_proof/`. The [Asset Forge static command](../../tools/willicat_asset_forge/SKILL.md) can inspect/package an isolated PNG for QA; **its `build-static` output crops to content and adds padding**, so its dimensions/pivot usually differ from this fixed-canvas contract. Do not drop that cropped output into a proof slot unregistered. Either use the approved exact-canvas RGBA source directly after alpha/edge QA, or explicitly re-register a Forge derivative to the contract through the existing production process and revalidate. No one-off processor and no silent world-coordinate changes. Rejected: opaque corners, clipped hairs/leaves/glow, wrong 3/4 perspective, painted time/weather/lamp effects, full-room scene, merged unrelated gameplay object, wrong pivot/scale or human style mismatch.
