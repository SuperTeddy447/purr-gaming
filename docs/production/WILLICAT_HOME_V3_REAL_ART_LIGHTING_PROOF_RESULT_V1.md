# WilliCat Home V3 real-art lighting proof V1 — result

Status: **FAIL / ART OR LIGHTING REVISION REQUIRED** for the final visual-production gate. The five dev-only samples import and the runtime lighting/capture system works, but the rendered [15-image evidence pack](../../artifacts/prototype_review/home_v3_real_art_lighting_proof_v1/README.md) does **not** show a coherent room-lighting proof. The prepared fixed roots were deliberately not moved. Production Home and the locked Home V3 gameplay layout were not modified. Assets remain **CANDIDATE**, not FINAL.

## Rendered atmosphere and visual verdict

The [neutral](../../artifacts/prototype_review/home_v3_real_art_lighting_proof_v1/01_neutral.png), [day](../../artifacts/prototype_review/home_v3_real_art_lighting_proof_v1/02_day_clear.png), [sunset](../../artifacts/prototype_review/home_v3_real_art_lighting_proof_v1/03_sunset_clear.png), [night OFF](../../artifacts/prototype_review/home_v3_real_art_lighting_proof_v1/04_night_lamp_off.png), [night ON](../../artifacts/prototype_review/home_v3_real_art_lighting_proof_v1/05_night_lamp_on.png) and [rain](../../artifacts/prototype_review/home_v3_real_art_lighting_proof_v1/06_rainy_afternoon.png) captures are real 540×960 Godot viewport images. Runtime CanvasModulate and lights make clear day/sunset/night differences without replacing any whole-room art. The character remains identifiable, but eyes and paw detail are small at default framing and the floor-contact shadow is visibly separate from the architecture plate. Night contrast is marginal at room view; [close review](../../artifacts/prototype_review/home_v3_real_art_lighting_proof_v1/08_character_close.png) is better but not a substitute for game scale.

The room composition is the decisive failure: the approved architecture is a small **opaque rectangular wall/floor plate** centered high in the greybox. The fixed window frame sits to its right as a freestanding object; the practical lamp sits below/left of the plate; the chair is far below. The cat straddles the plate edge and its contact shadow falls on the greybox. Thus this lab currently proves that the assets load and can be tinted, **not** that a registered Home V3 environment works under real lighting. No root, camera, gameplay marker, or locked layout coordinate was moved to hide this.

### Window

The **same** WindowSample texture remains assigned for [day](../../artifacts/prototype_review/home_v3_real_art_lighting_proof_v1/09a_window_day.png), [night](../../artifacts/prototype_review/home_v3_real_art_lighting_proof_v1/09b_window_night.png), [sunset](../../artifacts/prototype_review/home_v3_real_art_lighting_proof_v1/09c_window_sunset.png), [afternoon clear](../../artifacts/prototype_review/home_v3_real_art_lighting_proof_v1/09d_window_clear.png), and [same-time rain](../../artifacts/prototype_review/home_v3_real_art_lighting_proof_v1/09e_window_rain.png). Its panes have transparent source pixels and the separate controller changes exterior/rain values. However the current controller draws a rectangular colored backdrop larger than the small frame, and the rain strokes are not meaningfully legible at game scale. The window is detached from the architecture panel. Therefore **separate composition is technically present but not visually validated**.

### Lamp and shadows

One runtime `PointLight2D` changes with `FORCE_OFF`, `FORCE_ON`, and the existing `DIMMED` policy; no separate ON fixture image or baked light pool was introduced. In the two same-state night captures, mean absolute RGB difference is approximately `(80,64,39)` around the lamp/greybox, but only `(6,4,2)` over the architecture sample and near-zero over the cat. The practical light mostly illuminates placeholder floor, **not nearby real wall/character art**, so the lamp-material integration gate fails. The fixture itself reads when OFF and appears warm when ON.

Chair [before](../../artifacts/prototype_review/home_v3_real_art_lighting_proof_v1/10a_chair_shadow_before.png) and [after](../../artifacts/prototype_review/home_v3_real_art_lighting_proof_v1/10b_chair_shadow_moved.png) show the owner-local contact shadow following a +52 world-pixel temporary move; the chair root was restored and no painted stain remains. The protagonist also has an owner-local contact shadow. Both are crude grey blobs; their softness/placement is not yet a storybook-art approval. Zero shadow-enabled lights and zero occluders were used.

### Materials, style, and optional normal map

Cream plaster, jade paint, warm wood, and brass remain distinguishable in day and at character-close scale. The small chair's wood/jade/upholstery survives day but becomes hard to judge under night at default framing. The architecture source retains a warm neutral-looking base with no visible long sun streak; no source was repainted. The hard plate boundary and separated scene components break the intended illustrated-café coherence. No chair-specific normal map was supplied or generated, so no honest A/B chair verdict is possible. **Do not mandate a chair normal map**: the unmodified illustrated base remains legible where lit, while the previous atmosphere lab's different-asset trial found its simple normal response unconvincing. Revisit only with a separately authored chair normal map and real device comparison.

## Mobile and technical gates

The isolated proof uses **2 PointLight2D** nodes (lamp and window), **0 shadow-enabled lights**, **0 LightOccluder2D**, **0 weather particles**, **0 normal maps**, and **1 CanvasModulate** world tint. The HUD is a separate CanvasLayer. Captures used Godot 4.7.2's Metal Mobile renderer on an Apple M2 Pro; this is not a target phone benchmark. No p50/p95 frame-time, memory, thermal, battery or low/mid-tier device results are claimed. The existing F6 guard was not bypassed: it required all five exact canvas sizes and transparent perimeters; the new integration test also removes one texture to prove the guard rejects it.

Godot headless editor scan and 15-image GUI runtime capture completed. Every final PNG decoded at 540×960, was non-empty and was not single-color. No production Home scene, Foundation, gameplay, Home V3 spatial root, camera, or source image was modified. Final visual production must wait for human review and a deliberate asset-to-locked-layout registration strategy; **this task did not perform that redesign**.
