# WILLICAT NEXT ENVIRONMENT VISUAL PROOF PLAN V1

## 1. Objective
Design the next image-generation task to validate the **Hybrid Room-First Kit** approach and the course-correction toward a **softer, handmade storybook style**. 

This is a visual proof of coherence, *not* final production art for cutting. It must validate that we can generate a cohesive space that feels like WilliCat, without drifting into luxury architectural realism.

## 2. Proof Subject: "The Lived-In Café Corner"
Instead of generating an isolated wall, generate a focused, integrated diorama corner of the Home V3 Staggered Salon.

**Must Include:**
- The intersection of the floor and the back/side wall.
- The Signature Window (Right side).
- One staggered seating island (Table + 2 Chairs).
- Soft, natural daylight casting a gentle, painterly mood.
- (Optional for scale) A silhouette or loose sketch of a cat interacting with the space (e.g., sleeping on a chair).

## 3. Constraints & What Must Be Absent
- **NO UI elements.**
- **NO disjointed floating walls** (the environment must feel grounded and connected).
- **NO luxury hotel vibes, hyper-glossy surfaces, or sterile Art Deco.** (Avoid marble, excessive gold trim, and photorealistic lighting).
- **NO baked-in deep black shadows.** Keep the lighting neutral but warm.

## 4. Evaluation Questions
When the visual proof is generated, it will be evaluated against these criteria:
1. **Style Calibration:** Does it look like a high-end, cozy storybook illustration rather than an architectural render? Are the shapes large, readable, and slightly imperfect?
2. **Cohesion:** Do the floor, walls, and window feel like they exist in the same physical and atmospheric space?
3. **Camera Perspective:** Does it respect the elevated 3/4 isometric-lite camera angle without forced or warped perspective?
4. **Expansion Compatibility:** Does this style look like it can be modularized? (i.e., can we realistically separate the chairs and table from the background while maintaining the art style?)

## 5. Execution Handoff
This plan will be handed off to the Image Model / Concept Artist. They are instructed to generate 1-3 cohesive variations of "The Lived-In Café Corner" using the refined prompt parameters defined in this document.
