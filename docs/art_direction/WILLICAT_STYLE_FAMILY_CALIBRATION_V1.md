## 10. Contour / brush language

Readable painted silhouettes, selected thin warm/dark contours and grouped internal details define the family. Architecture has firmer joins; leaves/grass break their outline organically. Character and symbolic props can have slightly clearer contour than environment, without thick generic black cartoon lines. Internal sketch marks explain form, not uniformly outline every grain, fur strand or tile.

**PROPOSED:** preserve tactile brush variation and modest asymmetry; simplify microdetail at gameplay size. Reject sterile vector finish, glossy 3D, blurry watercolor wash, hyper-detailed realism and pixel art. No universal numerical line-width or detail quota is measured.

## 11. Lighting / shadow policy

**PROPOSED neutral source assumption:** soft broadly neutral illumination, subtle form shading, restrained intrinsic AO, material depth cues and tiny fixed construction shading. Lit lanterns/warm scene casts in references are contextual; future reusable sources must not inherit their global warmth or switched glow.

| Source art may contain | Runtime/context must own |
|---|---|
| Local form shading, small construction recesses, restrained material highlights | Golden hour/sunbeams, sunset/night/weather tint, switchable lamp illumination, scene atmosphere |

Separate contact shadows where appropriate, registered to the approved template. No character/movable-object directional cast shadows baked into reusable PNGs. No long sunlight casts or heavy universal drop shadow. No lighting rig, shadow coordinates or numeric intensity is selected here.
