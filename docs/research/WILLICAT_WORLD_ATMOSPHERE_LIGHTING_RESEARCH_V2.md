# WilliCat world atmosphere / lighting research V2

Status: Godot 4.7.2 architecture research for a **development lab**, not final art or measured mobile approval. Accessed 2026-09-29. The three sections below deliberately distinguish engine facts, physical-lighting references and our own art direction.

## Official Godot 4.7 facts

- Godot's [2D lighting guide](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/2d/2d_lights_and_shadows.html) describes `CanvasModulate`, `PointLight2D`, `DirectionalLight2D`, `LightOccluder2D`, `OccluderPolygon2D`, `CanvasTexture` normal/specular maps and SDF access. A background clear color does **not** receive 2D lighting; a drawn floor/background does. Larger point-light textures touch more pixels; shadow filters PCF5/PCF13 add cost. Additive sprites are cheaper light-like FX, but cannot illuminate very dark areas correctly, cast shadows or use normal/specular response.
- [`CanvasModulate`](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_canvasmodulate.html) colors a canvas and only one is active per canvas. [`CanvasLayer`](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_canvaslayer.html) renders independently; our existing HUD is a CanvasLayer, so the world can tint without dimming interface text. This must still be verified in a real rendered viewport.
- [`DirectionalLight2D`](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_directionallight2d.html) represents parallel rays; its **position is ignored**, rotation determines direction and it does not support item light-cull masks, though shadow-cull masks work. Godot's 2D guide explicitly says its built-in cast shadows are infinitely long. For this illustrated, mobile-first room we should disable directional shadows and use modest object/actor contact shadows; SDF or stylized custom long shadows remain an optional later experiment, not a dependency.
- [`PointLight2D`](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_pointlight2d.html) requires a light texture. A radial `GradientTexture2D` is supported by the 2D guide; a fixture-owned point light can move with its fixture. A selective local shadow would require [`LightOccluder2D`](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_lightoccluder2d.html) with an [`OccluderPolygon2D`](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_occluderpolygon2d.html). Light/occluder masks are deliberate ownership tools, not free performance fixes.
- Normal and specular maps on 2D elements use a [`CanvasTexture`](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/2d/2d_lights_and_shadows.html#normal-and-specular-maps). They can make glossy materials respond, but alter painted form; they are not automatically suitable for matte illustrated cats, textiles or plaster. Compare a neutral illustrated proxy with no map, a mild normal map and a restrained stylized shader before any pipeline mandate.
- [`CanvasItem` shaders](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/canvas_item_shader.html) have `light()` and render modes including `unshaded`. The 2D SDF is generated from light occluders when requested by shaders. A full-screen [screen-reading shader](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/shaders/screen-reading_shaders.html) triggers a back-buffer copy; do not make it the baseline weather/grade implementation on mobile. [`SubViewport`](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/rendering/viewports.html) can render the same room to distinct sizes for review, but another viewport has its own canvas/layer context and is not a free production post-process.
- Godot's [rendering-architecture documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/engine_details/architecture/internal_rendering_architecture.html) says 2D light rendering is single-pass in Forward+/Mobile and lights **with shadows** are much costlier than unshadowed ones; the Compatibility renderer uses additional passes for shadowed lights. `rendering_method="mobile"` is set in this project. These are cost directions, not a frame-time measurement for WilliCat.

## Real-world lighting research (not a real-time astronomy implementation)

- [NOAA's solar geometry glossary](https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/glossary.html) defines azimuth/elevation and explains latitude/season effects on solar paths; the [NOAA calculation note](https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/solareqns.PDF) relates latitude, declination and hour angle to solar elevation. Our profiles use **broad latitude bands and authored sun directions**, not exact dates/longitudes. The NOAA web calculator itself notes limited maintenance, so it is background reference rather than a production dependency.
- The [Met Office cloud-light explanation](https://weather.metoffice.gov.uk/learn-about/weather/types-of-weather/clouds/why-are-clouds-white) explains water-droplet scattering, reduced light under thick rain cloud, and warm low-sun color. This supports a weather modifier that lowers direct sun, raises the importance of diffuse ambient/lamp light and softens contrast, rather than replacing the location profile.
- [NASA Earth Observatory's aerosol overview](https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov/features/Aerosols) explains that aerosols may scatter **or absorb** sunlight and that humidity can change their behavior. Haze/humidity values here are stylized tendencies and must not be treated as a national color code or a claim about all weather in a country.
- A rainy/wet look is not merely blue tint: exterior visibility, glass streaks, lower direct contrast, lamp prominence and selective material highlights matter. This is an **art-direction inference** from the physical references and a design choice for WilliCat, not a quantitative climate forecast.

## WilliCat decisions from that evidence

The five composable axes are `LocationProfile`, `SeasonProfile`, `TimeOfDayProfile`, `WeatherProfile`, and optional `EventLightingModifier`. Country-named reference profiles are **scenario labels only**—Japan/Thailand/Europe/China do not prescribe cultural decor or a single national climate. In production, a location ID should denote an authored café region/local environment with a broad latitude/climate/window tendency, and allowed seasons must be validated. Time/weather/event change at runtime; no combination replaces the entire interior.

Render baseline: neutral illuminated base art + one CanvasModulate + one unshadowed DirectionalLight2D + a small number of fixture/window PointLight2Ds + object/actor-owned contact shadows + localized window/exterior/weather overlays. The current lab keeps shadow-enabled light and full-screen shader counts at zero. Event lighting adjusts evaluated values and can request local fixture/FX variants while preserving underlying state. Final art should contain form shading/AO but no permanent golden-hour streak, lamp pool, rain-dark whole-room layer or seasonal tint. See [lighting art contract](../art/WILLICAT_HOME_V3_LIGHTING_ART_CONTRACT_V2.md).

Mobile proof is incomplete without a minimum real phone: record p50/p95 frame time, GPU/CPU cost if available, thermal behavior, memory, draw calls, local light count, shadow/occluder count and transitions at 9:16/tall. The desktop Godot lab can prove composability/visual legibility and gather indicative numbers, not certify shipping performance.
