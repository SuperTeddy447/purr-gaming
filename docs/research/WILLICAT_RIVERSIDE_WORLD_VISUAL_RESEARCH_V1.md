## 1. Scope and evidence discipline

Current environment direction for this task is **real Stylized 3D Storybook Diorama**, with elevated 3/4 orthographic/near-orthographic presentation. Existing 2D characters/UI/FX may remain hybrid. The original [Style Calibration V1](../art_direction/WILLICAT_STYLE_FAMILY_CALIBRATION_V1.md) retains its historic 2D/runtime approval scope; this new task authorizes exterior concept exploration, not amendment of that record or production migration.

`DOCUMENTED` means a source states it. `OBSERVED` means the cited official imagery was viewed. `WILLICAT DESIGN INTERPRETATION` and `PROPOSED` mean our original decisions; they are not claims about Japan, another game or measured runtime geometry. Sources are public official institutions, creators or publishers. Tourism media overrepresent preserved/visitor-facing settings; Yanagawa municipal water-use context and mixed building details counter that bias. This is a limited desk study, not a field survey or real-world engineering design.

## 3. Visual and spatial principles discovered

| Evidence | Research finding | WILLICAT DESIGN INTERPRETATION |
|---|---|---|
| R1/R2 | Small water corridor and craft/frontage sequence coexist | Frame a calm home lane with modest shop fronts; the café is a neighbor, not a palace |
| R3/R4 | Waterways have commercial/drainage/daily-life histories and maintenance | Give stone banks, a drain mouth, maintenance steps and bridge approaches functional relationships |
| R5 | Mixed ordinary roof/wall forms, utility details and planted edges coexist | Include one quiet residential façade and one modern utility detail among cedar/plaster shops |
| R6 | Preserved commercial buildings still host local work | Propose a small bread/ceramics neighbor rather than a tourist souvenir strip |
| R7 | Flow, microtopography, ecology and lived history form one landscape | Let bank shape, small height transitions and planting follow the water; avoid arbitrary rocks/steps |
| G1 | Softened forms and vegetation clusters reinforce miniature reading | Keep roofs, walls and river masses broad; concentrate detail at entrances/bridges |
| G2/G3 | Compact exploratory landscape can mix marked routes and optional discoveries | Provide one legible home route plus a short optional nook; do not design a giant empty district |
| G4 | Repeated façade rhythm and water negative space read as a coherent kit | Share module connectors/materials; vary silhouettes without inventing a new grammar per building |
| G5 | Painted color hierarchy can supply material personality | Use quiet broad albedo variation, not photo-grain or full-scene golden glow |

These interpretations combine sources; no single real location or proprietary layout will be reproduced. Tiny Glade's castle vocabulary and Townscaper's candy-color identity are not adopted.

## 4. Riverside construction vocabulary

**PROPOSED families**, with geometry values **UNMEASURED** until a future authorized template extraction:

| Family | Spatial job | Design constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Stone retaining bank + warm cap | Separate walkable lane and water | Continuous top contact/bank-to-ground join; no decorative gaps pretending to be crossings |
| Lower natural bank | Change to a softer edge away from commerce | Planting patches follow recesses; keep water silhouette legible |
| Maintenance stair / landing | Explain access toward water | Locally framed recess and end caps; non-walkable vs walkable role explicitly resolved later |
| Drainage detail | Connect built ground to water | Small restrained outlet in bank, not an arbitrary dark hole on every piece |
| Pedestrian rail / terminal / corner | Frame exposed edge and bridge | Posts and horizontal rail rhythm remain readable; no tooth-like dense pickets |
| Bridge deck / abutment / approaches | Make an actual continuous crossing | Calm low rise, readable entrance/exit, no oversized fantasy arch |
| Ground seam / curb / threshold | Mark street → café → bank relationships | Clear walking surface; no false raised obstacle implied by paint |
| Storefront / residence | Supply everyday neighborhood rhythm | Cedar/cream proportions, simple roof, varied ordinary windows; utility detail used selectively |

This is a visual/game kit vocabulary. It does not certify flood/accessibility/building-code compliance.

## 5. Landscape composition

**WILLICAT DESIGN INTERPRETATION:** three depth bands, not three separate side-scrolling layers:

- **Foreground:** player-accessible calm path; a low vegetation frame; no large canopy concealing the entry.
- **Midground:** home café as warm landmark, readable river edge and one bridge; nearby fronts share scale.
- **Background:** low-contrast distant house/tree/hill masses continue the world. They are noninteractive visual continuation until future scope approval, not implied fully playable geometry.

Water supplies a quiet negative-space ribbon. Building clusters frame route reveals. A bend offers a partial view beyond the bridge; keep the crossing's two approaches visible. No skyline/horizon-heavy cinematic composition, shallow DOF, fog or giant UI will conceal design problems.

## 6. Mobile camera and readability

**PROPOSED common camera family:** portrait 9:16, elevated 3/4, orthographic/near-orthographic, soft isometric-lite miniature gameplay. Exact elevation/azimuth/world coverage are not locked by a generated concept. There is no strict diamond tile requirement.

Inspect the Master at 360×640 and 540×960 as design previews: entrance separation, continuous primary path, both bridge approaches, exits and actor contrast. Decorative joints/flower dots that vanish must not carry navigation or interaction meaning. A static image test does not establish touch target size, movement/Y-sort correctness, 3D occlusion, mobile GPU performance or human acceptance.

Canonical orange protagonist remains separate identity authority. Concept scenery will not generate a substitute protagonist. Later authorized runtime proof must use the existing actor and show movement/depth interactions. Readability can be examined with a clearly labeled conceptual actor-size overlay in the review viewer, without changing original concept PNGs or claiming calibrated world scale.

## 7. World DNA extending café identity

**PROPOSED:** honey/cedar framing; warm cream plaster; subdued moss/sage; warm gray stone; blue-green water; charcoal/blue-gray roof; small aged-brass/ceramic accents. “Muted jade” in the task is a restrained optional green relationship, not a new mandatory palette or Art Deco revival. Existing measured palette remains authority; exterior variation is not a new measured calibration.

Weathering is clean pigment/value variation at edges/recesses, not grime. Shared softened profiles and painted broad material patches connect interior/exterior. Neighborhood buildings can mix old timber/plaster with quieter ordinary infill. Street detail carries everyday work: pots by a doorway, one bicycle element, a covered crate/bench nook. Leave route centers calm.

**Original WilliCat identity opportunities:** a small double-notch cedar rail terminal, quiet rounded ceramic planter lips, paired leaf-mass silhouettes, cream frontage with a single muted green awning, and a small abstract paw/bean emblem supplied separately at runtime. These are proposals, not a claim of cultural tradition. Do not bake readable text, giant cat faces or symbolic collectible icons into every architectural object.

## 8. Clichés and mistakes to avoid

No torii/pagoda/temple/giant-kanji/lantern/cherry-blossom saturation. No unbroken historical museum street or tourism-boat district. No stone/flower detail on every pixel, identical trees, glossy water, huge empty floor, random elevation, railings through routes or a bridge without legible approaches. Do not mistake a painted 3D-looking concept for a mesh, modular kit, production-ready asset or validated playable layout.

## 9. Three concepts to explore after this synthesis

| Concept | Independent spatial idea | Risk to inspect |
|---|---|---|
| A — Home Riverside Lane | Intimate café frontage, short pedestrian lane and close water relationship | Straight corridor may feel narrow/repetitive |

| C — Neighborhood Waterway | Mixed residence/commerce, pocket garden, side routes | Too many exit branches may confuse or feel crowded |

Master will synthesize A's domestic frontage, B's readable bend/crossing and C's ordinary side-route logic. It will not copy one unchanged. Conceptual future connections do not overwrite the continuous Home graph (Café ↔ Riverside, Plaza below, Garden above).

## 10. Living details — proposed mechanisms only

| Motion | Candidate mechanism | Review rule |
|---|---|---|
| Slow water/current marks | Shared shader UV flow / restrained normal-free color marks | Match bank/current direction; avoid sparkling noise |
