To regenerate the 15 atmosphere images (01–13, 15–16): open `res://scenes/dev/world_atmosphere_lighting_lab_v2.tscn` in Godot, Run Current Scene, press F6. To regenerate the material image: open `res://scenes/dev/atmosphere/material_response_probe.tscn`, Run Current Scene, press F6. The atmosphere scene also supports `--capture-atmosphere` in a rendered run and quits after capturing. Headless Godot cannot provide `frame_post_draw` here, so use a real rendered window/editor for these PNGs.

Visual QA notes: the first capture showed clear-morning washout, so the **lab renderer**'s directional contribution was restrained and all 13 images regenerated; no source art/layout was altered. Greybox labels and tiny actor placeholders remain prototype-quality. The fixed window exterior is a simple pane color/rain overlay, not a final authored view; do not infer art approval. Material B changed/darkened the chair's painted volume without a clear gain; no normal-map mandate follows. Final Mochi art and low-end phone readability/performance have not been tested.
